## Full description (max. 4000)

```
Learn & Play is an educational app for kids ages 3 to 8. With Owlito, a friendly owl, your child practices addition, subtraction and Spanish through play, at their own pace and with no pressure.

WHAT YOUR CHILD LEARNS
• Addition up to 5, 10 and 20, with pictures to count.
• Subtraction with pictures that get crossed out, to see how many are left.
• Spanish: animals, fruits, colors, numbers, family, body, clothes, greetings, food, house, school, weather and actions.
• A memory game to remember the Spanish words.

A VOICE THAT TEACHES
Owlito reads every question out loud. If your child makes a mistake, Owlito calmly explains the answer by counting together, so they learn from the mistake instead of getting frustrated. Spanish words are spoken by a native Spanish voice.

A NEW CHALLENGE EVERY DAY
Every day there is a "Today's challenge" with new questions and extra stars. The stars for each level start over every day, so there is always something to win. If a day goes by without playing, Owlito gets a little sad… and cheers up when your child comes back!

STARS AND OWLITO'S SHOP
Every answer that is right on the first try earns a star. With their stars, kids buy hats, sunglasses, scarves and colorful feathers to dress up Owlito.

FOR MOMS AND DADS
• Progress summary: days played, games and the words that are hardest.
• Up to 4 kids on the same phone, each with their own progress.
• Optional daily reminder at the time you choose.
• The parents' area and purchases are protected by a grown-ups question.
• At night the app switches to soft colors.
• The app also works in Spanish (to learn English).

SAFE FOR KIDS
• No sign-up, no accounts and no chat.
• Progress is saved only on the phone.
• Ads are suitable for kids and are not personalized.

FREE AND PREMIUM
The app is free, with some levels and topics open. Premium is a one-time payment with no subscription: it unlocks all levels and topics and removes ads.

Privacy policy: https://vl4n.github.io/Infantil/privacidad.html#english
```
