## Full description (max. 4000)

```
Learn & Play is an educational app for kids ages 3 to 8. With Owlito, a friendly owl, your child practices addition, subtraction, numbers and Spanish through play, at their own pace and with no pressure.

WHAT YOUR CHILD LEARNS
• Addition and subtraction up to 5, 10 and 20, with pictures to count.
• Numbers: "What comes next?", counting by 2s and "Which is bigger?".
• Spanish: 17 topics, like animals, fruits, colors, numbers, family, body, clothes, food, house, school, weather, vehicles, toys, sea animals and feelings.
• A memory game to remember the Spanish words.

A VOICE THAT TEACHES
Owlito reads every question out loud. If your child makes a mistake, Owlito calmly explains the answer by counting together, so they learn from the mistake instead of getting frustrated. Spanish words are spoken by a native Spanish voice.

A REASON TO COME BACK EVERY DAY
• Today's challenge: new questions every day and extra stars.
• Daily medal: for finishing 5 games. Medals move your child along the Medal Path to win special prizes for Owlito.
• Sticker album: a new sticker for every game finished.
• If a day goes by without playing, Owlito gets a little sad… and cheers up when your child comes back!

OWLITO'S SHOP
With their stars, kids buy hats, sunglasses, headphones and colorful feathers to dress up Owlito.

FOR MOMS AND DADS
• Weekly summary: days played, games, right answers and medals.
• The words that are hardest, to practice together.
• Up to 4 kids on the same phone, each with their own progress.
• Optional daily reminder at the time you choose.
• The parents' area and purchases are protected by a grown-ups question.
• At night the app switches to soft colors.
• The app also works in Spanish (to learn English).

SAFE FOR KIDS
• No sign-up, no accounts and no chat.
• Progress is saved only on the phone.
• Ads are suitable for kids, are not personalized and never appear in the middle of an exercise.

FREE AND PREMIUM
The app is free, with some levels, topics and number games open. Premium is a one-time payment with no subscription: it unlocks all levels and topics, special outfits and more album pages, and removes ads.

Privacy policy: https://vl4n.github.io/Infantil/privacidad.html#english
```
